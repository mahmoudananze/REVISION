4 · Writing workshop

Task 1.1 · Compare two viewpoints (15 marks)

Write 4–5 sentences in your own words. Do not insert your opinion unless asked.

  1. Introduce the shared issue.
  2. Explain Viewpoint A and its main reason.
  3. Explain Viewpoint B and its main reason.
  4. Identify an important similarity.
  5. State the central difference.

Sentence support: Both viewpoints discuss … / Supporters argue that … / In contrast, critics believe … / Both sides recognise … / The main difference is …

Task 1.2 · Comparative essay (25 marks)

Write at least 220 words, answer all three prompts, and use paragraphs. A useful practice target is 240–280 words.

Introduction

Introduce both options and your opinion.

Body paragraph 1

Compare one feature of both options, with an example.

Body paragraph 2

Compare another feature and explain the effect.

Body paragraph 3

Justify your preference with a realistic example.

Conclusion

Summarise and confirm your judgement.

Useful connectors: both, similarly, whereas, while, however, in contrast, because, for example, on balance, in my view.

Choose a theme: Smart Homes OR Comparing Places.

Smart Homes · prompts and planning

Compare a highly automated home with a mainly conventional home. Discuss convenience and running costs; explain which type you prefer and why; support your choice with examples.

Compare convenience, costs, privacy, reliability and accessibility.

Comparing Places · planning

Compare megacities and small towns using the same criteria: education, jobs, transport, environment, community and affordability. Explain which setting you prefer and why.
